## stock
Crowd psychology: VIX, distance from the 52-week high, momentum (get_sentiment with asset_type "stock" and the ticker), and FOMO/panic tone in headlines (get_news). Extremes are contrarian signals; moderate momentum tends to persist.
## crypto
Crypto Fear & Greed index, euphoria vs capitulation, distance from 52-week high, RSI (get_sentiment with asset_type "crypto" and the ticker), and headline tone (get_news). Extreme fear is often a contrarian buy, extreme greed a warning.
## gold
Safe-haven demand: VIX and risk-off stress, gold's distance from its high, momentum (get_sentiment with asset_type "gold"), headline tone (get_news).
